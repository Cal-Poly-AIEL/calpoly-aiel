Daylin Atwood

B.A. Political Science, Minors: Computer Science and Dance ’26

Public Policy and Current Events Officer

Daylin is a 4th year political science student looking forward to attending law school next fall. She has spent her time at Cal Poly researching technology and public policy topics ranging from the implications of authoritarian surveillance programs to current levels of federal cybersecurity preparedness. She views AI ethics as a crucial consideration for the government agencies or companies she hopes to work with one day and hopes that the AI Ethics Lab can prepare her to thoughtfully navigate these relationships. Daylin is excited to facilitate dialogue between students entering a variety of different careers on how technology can be deployed safely and ethically.
